Welcome to Monkey Mart (accessible via https://rocket-goal.bitbucket.io/). Your privacy is a top priority for the Monkey Mart team. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and enjoy the playful world of Monkey Mart — where monkeys run the cutest tropical store! 🌴

1. Information We Collect

We keep data collection minimal and transparent. While using Monkey Mart, we may collect:

  • Game progress & preferences – saved locally in your browser (localStorage) to remember your in-game bananas, upgrades, and Mart layout. No personal identifiers are linked.
  • Anonymous usage statistics – like page visits, feature interactions, via privacy-friendly analytics (no cookies for tracking across web).
  • Technical data – browser type, device OS, and screen resolution for optimizing Monkey Mart game performance.

We do not collect real names, email addresses, IP addresses as personal identification, nor do we require registration to play Monkey Mart.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data helps us improve the Monkey Mart universe:

  • ✅ Keep your game progress saved (monkey coins, outfits, store level).
  • ✅ Analyze which features are most-loved to develop better updates.
  • ✅ Ensure the website runs smoothly and fix bugs across devices.
  • ✅ Never sell, rent, or share any data with third-party advertisers.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

Monkey Mart uses local storage (similar to cookies but for saving game data) to remember your in-game achievements and settings. We do not use tracking cookies, fingerprinting, or any cross-site tracking. You can clear local storage anytime via your browser settings — this will reset your Monkey Mart progress, but not affect your ability to play.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted on GitLab Pages (gitlab.io) and may use essential CDN services. These platforms operate under strict data protection standards. We do not embed external trackers, advertising networks, or social media plugins. Monkey Mart remains ad-free and focused purely on fun.

5. Children’s Privacy

Monkey Mart is a family-friendly game suitable for all ages. We never knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Because we collect zero personally identifiable information, no special consent is required, but parents are encouraged to supervise online gaming activities.

6. Your Rights & Control

Since we don’t store personal data in databases, you have full control via your browser: clear local storage / site data at any time. For any privacy inquiries about Monkey Mart, contact us directly through our GitLab repository or the contact widget inside the game hub.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date. Continuing to use Monkey Mart means you acknowledge the latest privacy terms.
